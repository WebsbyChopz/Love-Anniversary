♡ A LETTER FOR ARUN ♡

My favourite person,

If I could put everything I feel into one letter, I still don't think these words would be enough.

Since 19 October 2023, you've become part of so many little corners of my life — the first person I want to tell things to, the name that can change my mood, and the person I miss in moments when I wish the distance would just disappear.

Long distance has taught us patience. Our happy moments have given us reasons to smile, and even the difficult moments have things to teach us. I know love isn't just sweet words or cute surprises. It's respect, trust, effort, understanding, and choosing to talk even when it's easier to stay quiet.

And yes, sometimes we may get a little jealous or possessive because the person matters so much. But I hope we always turn that feeling into reassurance, honesty, and care — never fear. I want us to feel loved and free at the same time.

Arun, thank you for being part of this story. Thank you for the calls, the laughs, the little updates, the memories, and the moments still waiting for us. If I could go back to the beginning knowing everything I know now, I'd still want to discover this story with you.

We may not be able to hold hands whenever we want, but you still have a place in my heart that distance can't reach. One day, I hope we'll look back at all these miles and smile at how far we came.

“The best kind of love isn't about never having hard days. It's about being gentle with each other through them.”

Here's to more firsts, more favourite memories, more silly moments, more honest conversations, and a future we build one day at a time.

I choose you — today, and in all the little tomorrows. ♡
